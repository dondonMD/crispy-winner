# Measurement and replay

Every priced signal records features, scores, costs, state, mode, session and immutable configuration hash. Forward horizons: 15/30/60/180/300/600/1800 seconds. Entry observation starts only after latency; later observations measure returns, MFE/MAE and after-cost results. A gap beyond freshness tolerance marks that horizon unavailable; absent liquidity also makes it unavailable. These are estimates, not executable fills.

Evaluation selects the first structurally eligible signal for each mint/session before looking at outcomes, and reports due signals, measured samples, missing outcomes and coverage. Calibration/validation/holdout are the earliest 50%, next 25%, later 25% of that fixed chronological cohort. Mean intervals use normal approximations; correlation and regime shifts can invalidate naive confidence. Medians use a bounded deterministic reservoir of up to 5,000 outcomes. Additive return drawdown is labelled separately from dollar paper-account drawdown. Profit factor without losses is unavailable, not infinity.

Record-time eligibility prevents later information from changing which signals qualified. Evaluation is descriptive; it does not tune thresholds. New settings produce a new hash. Do not repeatedly test thresholds and select the best holdout. Positive labels require sample size, not a few green trades. A frozen evidence record requires positive validation AND holdout and complete first-signal outcome coverage. It does not eliminate selection bias, dependence, regime change or provider delay.

Replay uses normalized events in recorded order, a temporary SQLite database, the same feature/state/risk/paper pipeline, startup context, and contemporaneous provider/resource flags. It reads no later outcomes or remote providers. Reports score, decision/reason and state mismatches. Events older than raw retention cannot establish full replay equivalence. Restarted feature windows warm again; historical prices are not marked current. Out-of-order events are excluded and degrade the affected candidate.

No-trade baseline is $20 unchanged / zero net return. A naive graduation strategy and latency-sensitivity model are not represented as validated baselines in this version. The normal paper account is an actual ledger, not a sum of overlapping hypothetical signal returns. Collect independent launches across multiple market periods before considering evidence useful.
