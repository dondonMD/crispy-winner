# Fail-closed risk and costs

`evaluate_candidate` is the central entry gate. Paper entry requests and delayed fills both check its result. Critical mint/freeze authorities, unknown programs, dangerous transfer extensions, concentration and creator selling cannot be overridden by scores. Token-2022 metadata extensions are observations, not scam labels. Unsupported extensions/absent parser fields remain uncertain. Holder scans group token accounts by owner, exclude only derived curve/system and validated PumpSwap pool/vault accounts. Creator balances additionally use getTokenAccountsByOwner; top-20 distribution still does not establish a full wallet census.

Stale price, flow, liquidity or security; unhealthy providers; resource/storage pressure; dropped/out-of-order data; source disagreement; no independently verified pool; insufficient recovery/breadth/evidence: WAIT. Excessive chase: MISSED. Critical risks: REJECT. Rejected tokens cannot reopen an entry, but open paper positions retain monitoring priority for exit attempts.

TradeCostEstimator owns all fees. Dated conservative default: 1.25% DEX per side; optional platform fee 0%; $0.005 network and $0.005 priority per side; $0.01 failed-transaction reserve per round trip; 0.5% adverse slippage per side. These are explicit USD assumptions, not current execution quotes. Users must update them for their venue, SOL price and fee environment. PumpSwap fees vary by market-cap tiers and custom pools can differ. Constant-product impact uses half reported USD liquidity as approximate quote reserve, not an audited route. No transaction is constructed or submitted.

Break-even is solved using both entry and exit costs. Scenarios show gross move beside net P&L. A 25/50/100% exit assessment uses the proposed small position. The application does not invent a price target or maximum entry when evidence is absent. Historical net expectancy and risk-adjusted expectancy are unavailable until evidence exists. Transfer fees/hooks and exotic reserve configurations are not quoted as safe zero-cost paths.

Paper fills use the NEXT observable price after ≥250ms configured latency (default 500ms). Slippage and impact worsen fills; explicit fees debit cash. Exit liquidity is observed at exit, not copied from entry. Missing prices/liquidity delay exits; there is no guaranteed stop fill. Account state and journal commit atomically and survive restart. DEMO and real accounts are isolated.
