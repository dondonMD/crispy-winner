CRISPY WINNER implements useful launch/lifecycle monitoring, explicit evidence quality, observed trade statistics, conservative risk screening and isolated manual research/journalling. It does **not** yet establish that its early rankings predict superior executable after-cost returns. Nor can its core liquidity-based cost estimate establish that a user's sell transaction will succeed. EARLY_OPPORTUNITY, HIGH attention, CURRENT trade evidence and ENTRY_CANDIDATE have different meanings; none is a guarantee of safety or profit.

Two independent decision tracks must remain separate:

- **Opportunity Intelligence:** uncalibrated attention ranking, largely based on repeated curve progression. It can intentionally show EARLY_OPPORTUNITY without CURRENT trade flow or verified security. It neither approves a strict entry nor supplies a calibrated entry/exit price.
- **Strict core / FOMO Companion:** a post-graduation strategy with verified/current security, concentration, clean buyer flow, market structure, economics and statistical-approval gates. Companion rechecks and never upgrades a WAIT into an entry. Pre-grad EARLY cannot bypass these requirements.

A confirmed audit finding needs priority review: the legacy CLI can create an `evidence` approval using retrospective splits, while engine startup accepts a version/mode-matching record without checking prospective provenance. This conflicts with the intended prospective-only approval policy. No approval was issued or database inspected for approval contents during this audit; the finding concerns the reachable implementation, not a claim of actual misuse.

Status vocabulary throughout: **FUNCTIONAL** means a traceable implemented behavior with relevant deterministic coverage, not a proven predictive edge; **PARTIAL** means limited/estimated evidence or an incomplete capability; **BLOCKED** means an established prerequisite is absent or a known incompatibility prevents the claimed use; **UNVERIFIED** means runtime evidence for the present source/environment is missing. Historical successes are explicitly historical.

## 3. Early-opportunity discovery capabilities

### How the early ranking actually works

The default [intelligence configuration](../../config/intelligence.yaml) uses curve freshness180s, minimum progress4 percentage points over at least60s, pullback4pp, collapse25pp. curve_dynamics uses at most the stored verified curve history within1800s, not a reconstructed full market history. It computes net/prior progression,120s/600s velocity, acceleration where spacing permits, positive-change persistence, retreat from session peak and time in regions below30/below85/85+.

The EARLY predicate requires current matching latest curve evidence, at least3 samples, at least60s coverage, net progression>=4pp, persistence>=.75, INCREASING quality, retreat<=3pp, and either current verified security or at least4 samples/120s coverage. Hard security failures, core REJECT, rejected/expired state, provider disagreement or observed creator selling yield AVOID first. Missing/stale curve yields DATA_LIMITED; a prior rise with retreat4..less-than25pp and enough observations can yield PULLBACK_WATCH; severe retreat yields DATA_LIMITED. Remaining cases DEVELOPING. Core MISSED maps to TOO_EXTENDED.

Ranking components: persistence up to25, net progression20, sample count10, freshness15, controlled retreat10, current security10, current trade evidence5, market evidence5. HIGH attention comes from EARLY, not a calibrated expected return. The warning explicitly says curve progression is not price growth. entry_context.review_zone and maximum_chase are unavailable; no executable entry range or target is invented.

**Important scope:** the Opportunity action itself does not impose curve<85 or graduation-is-None. test_high_curve_alone_not_too_extended_but_core_missed_is explicitly expects EARLY at90→96. The separate early-edge research signal_snapshot adds pre-grad, curve<85, canonical known Pump units, readiness and risk filters. Neither scope should be silently substituted for the other in UI interpretation or research reporting.

### Strong evidence versus temporary hype

Repeated, timed verified progression distinguishes this heuristic from a one-point curve jump. It does not prove broad durable demand. Genuine buyer-flow enrichment requires observed canonical trades with known units/current continuity, not DEX rolling m5 counts. window_features measures observed buy/sell counts, independent-buyer estimate, persistence, whale share, repeat buyers and acceleration; score_token combines breadth, flow, persistence/distribution and cluster/creator/volatility penalties into uncalibrated maturity/momentum scores.

Coordination detection is limited to same rounded quote size within2s shared by at least3 wallets. It discounts estimated breadth and reports POSSIBLY RELATED WALLETS; it neither proves ownership nor detects all coordinated trades. whale_share is the largest observed buying wallet's share of observed buy volume, not holder concentration or total beneficial ownership. Creator sell detection uses direct address equality and only visible accepted evidence. Positive curve motion can therefore coexist with missing buyer evidence, unseen creator-linked selling or clustered demand.

### Overextension and crowding

Core chase score combines observed price move above15%, extension above5% versus trade-price VWAP, and whale share above.4; configured chase>=60 gives MISSED unless hard risks already reject. Companion maps MISSED to TOO_LATE. A high curve alone is intentionally not TOO_EXTENDED. Pump TradeEvents normally supply quote volume rather than USD price, so absent reliable priced trades/VWAP limits those price-based chase terms; a zero/default statistic must not be interpreted as proof the token is unextended. Even a DEX USD price is not a fill quote.

The isolated early-edge design separately records first verified curve>=85 and first CURRENT estimated buyers>=existing min_buyers20, plus graduation, as descriptive crowd markers. They are not a validated definition of social attention or an entry command. Primary results must include tokens never reaching these markers; conditioning on graduates would create survivorship bias.

## 4. Strict entry and risk-gate capabilities

[evaluate_candidate](../app/economics.py) has three classes of blocker:

- **Hard REJECT:** explicit security hard failures, observed creator sell, top1>10% or top5>30% independent-owner concentration, unacceptable configured estimated impact/slippage. Unknowns are not converted to passing zeros.
- **Evidence/quality WAIT:** required current continuous trade evidence, security quality/TTL300s, creator identity/holdings, concentration, eligible state, price/trade/liquidity within20s, provider health, nondegraded resources, independent post-grad pool verification, agreement, confirmed graduation,60s post-grad wait, pullback/stabilisation/renewed structure, buyers>=20, known consistent units, maturity>=65, momentum>=70, liquidity>=$10,000 and viable costs/size. Missing data and a hard risk are distinct.
- **Statistical/economic WAIT:** no validated after-cost edge, insufficient positive approved evidence, no cost/exitability estimate or sizing margin. Approval requires count>=100 and positive lower CI in the evaluator, plus configured min_edge_pct3 in sizing. This is intended protection, not a reason to lower gates.

Core gives REJECT before MISSED before WAIT before ALLOW_PAPER_ENTRY. Research eligibility can ignore the named missing-edge waits but still requires the other gates. Strict research eligibility is therefore not Opportunity's broad EARLY predicate. Companion's ENTRY_CANDIDATE requires stored core approval plus successful fresh evaluator recheck, finite structured trade evidence, known positive viable size and complete current market/security/history. HIGH attention can still accompany WAIT near graduation. Neither Companion nor the manual journal signs or executes anything.

Before graduation the system can know observed curve history, canonical Pump flow and any acquired mint/creator/distribution checks. It cannot claim a migrated pool, fresh post-grad liquidity/price structure or PumpSwap buyer window. At migration engine._process preserves the chain occurrence time but resets flow epoch to max(processing time,event time), clears last_trade and excludes pre-grad trades from post-grad features. PumpSwap ACK starts pool coverage;180 clean seconds,>=3 samples, consistent units, freshness and no relevant gap are still required. Restart, transport loss and relevant gaps never pre-earn this window.

Security limitations matter independently of strict thresholds: largest-account owner grouping is not a whole-holder/beneficial-owner census; exclusions are known curve/pool/vault accounts, not universal LP custody/burn verification. Creator direct holdings improve on the top20 proxy but do not enumerate related wallets. parse_pool validates program owner/discriminator/base mint/stable account prefix; that alone does not validate a user's route, token account, immutable liquidity or all future upgrade behavior. Security scans use sequential confirmed requests rather than the research oracle's single coherent pricing batch; cross-slot concentration/denominator drift needs explicit validation before treating the scan as a consistent census.

## 5. Market-data and provider limitations

[engine enrichment](../app/engine.py) batches hot curves, sweeps background candidates, requests DEX markets only for graduated deep candidates, and scans one prioritized eligible security candidate per global interval. No global full-universe continuous inspection is implemented. Defaults:250 candidates,25 deep,500 events/token,1,000 admission queue,100 selected/batch,250ms cadence, hot curve30s, background batch25/30s and target revisit180s, security TTL300s and global scan interval30s. Backoff/resource pressure can extend waits.

Two capacity implications are projections from code, not new live measurements:25 continuously due security candidates need at least750s for one scan each at one/30s, exceeding300s TTL;225 background candidates need at least9 batches/270s without repeats/errors, exceeding Opportunity's180s freshness if all remain background. Existing prioritization may serve selected tokens well, but cannot establish equal observation opportunity for all candidates. Do not raise TTL or limits to disguise this. Early-edge readiness/coverage tracks counts, duration, largest gaps, hot/background exposure, provider pressure and capacity censorship; fair-control availability remains a practical prerequisite.

DEX Screener supplies cached15s USD prices/liquidity and reported market cap or FDV for selected PumpSwap pairs. Provider_market accepts only explicitly sourced, timely price and true MARKET_CAP, not launch metadata or FDV as actual cap. Independent pool parsing validates pair identity; it does **not** independently verify the displayed USD liquidity or guarantee executable reserves. Provider disagreement checks contemporaneous source prices differing by>15%; when only one comparable USD source exists, no disagreement is not independent agreement.

Pump logs use confirmed commitment, not finalized irreversible trade execution. Explicit Log truncated triggers bounded standard getTransaction recovery, not ordinary trade polling. Recovery2/32/45s/3 remains fail closed: untracked-confirmed clears pending, affected tracked mints gap locally, unresolved/unsupported/overflow/expiry causes conservative coverage reset. Malformed unknown identity, queue loss, stale processing, buffer rolloff within180s and transport failure remain different accounted loss paths. Pool-specific truncation resets only its token; shared PumpSwap disconnect gaps all subscribed pools. ACK proves subscription acceptance, not notifications or completeness.

The [capacity ADR](adr-evidence-pipeline-capacity-20261025.md) preserves passing and failing finite trials. Repeated50/s no-growth is not established; some runs have complete eventual persistence and low p99 while failing the unchanged backlog rule. A previous600s run had19,317/30,000 persisted,9,959 stale,698 drops and26 explicitly untracked. Earlier real six-hour infrastructure censorship, long observation gap, recovery loss, zero sampled CURRENT and four unmatched qualifying EARLY observations remain historical failures, not current usable prospective evidence. The approximately1GiB storage guard/retention and censorship receipts bound resources but do not make removed/missing observations complete. No current session was sampled in this audit.

Known PumpSwap8-byte suffix incompatibility remains blocked pending authoritative layout; strict full-consumption parsing is correct fail-closed behavior. The official Pump4.2.0 quote_transfer_fee fix is a different, authoritatively resolved event and does not explain PumpSwap's suffix. No program-wide subscription, opaque-tail acceptance or DEX-count flow substitute is recommended.

|Impact|notional/(liquidityUSD/2+notional)|Balanced constant-product approximation; liquidityUSD is provider-reported. Curve/boosted/route-specific reserve behavior is not proved by this formula.|
|Fee verification label|2026-10-08 string|Provenance label only; does not execute fee verification or establish current interface pricing.|
|Price/liquidity|Timestamped DEX observations when available|Market context, not an order-book/route guarantee or exact user's net proceeds.|

For a$4 flat-price round trip, assumed fees=$.10, slippage=$.04, network=$.01, priority=$.01, failed reserve=$.01: **$.17/4=4.25% plus modeled price impact**. Platform contributes zero under this config. Impact varies with liquidity; break-even is numerically solved with exit fee/notional changes, so4.25% is not an exact break-even quote. No executable target is supplied: maximum_acceptable_entry remains unavailable.

The collector uses entry and exit liquidity where observed; manual exit uses current liquidity for an approximate scenario. Costs are estimates applied to price changes, not observed fills. Price impact is deliberately charged in this approximation; it must not also be charged on top of a future SDK quote that already embeds it. Core/manual model does not establish ATA rent, account setup/closure, funding/wrapping/route-specific costs or actual interface charges. Do not interpret exitability.score or fractional liquidation scenarios as simulated sell success.

## 7. Historical and prospective evidence limitations

OutcomeCollector freezes existing features/scores/risk/price/liquidity/decision/version/size. It selects the next observed price after configured latency500ms, collects horizon exits and after-cost estimates, MFE/MAE and delays, and preserves unavailable capacity/cost/gap/no-price cases. It is an observational price model, not the10s executable-state early-edge instrument. A delayed first entry price is not subject to the early instrument's explicit entry acquisition window; do not describe the two as equivalent manual-entry evidence.

Retrospective evaluate defaults300s, deduplicates one token/session/horizon and uses chronological50/25/25 splits, reporting missingness/capacity and uncertainty warnings. These splits are descriptive; correlated tokens, heavy tails, selection, survivorship, overlapping exposure, regimes and multiple testing remain. A positive sample mean or normal CI is not independently validated profitability.

OpportunityResearch records action transitions, provider-only baselines and descriptive future curve/price/cap observations. Manual context is excluded from research baselines. Curve gains and market-cap changes are not token PnL; costed price-based results still use the approximation. Repeated transitions do not automatically become independent primary samples or executable-price outcomes.

Strict ProspectiveCohort freezes git/config/mode/version,300s primary, earliest eligible token/session, immutable snapshots and DB-backed pending/quality data; drift refuses append and unavailable outcomes remain in the denominator. The retired cohort must stay unchanged and cannot be revived by this audit. Its missing/censored observations cannot be backfilled or promoted into prospective validation.

**Confirmed approval enforcement gap:** cli.main freeze-evidence calls retrospective evaluate, checks validation/holdout status and coverage, then db.add("evidence",...). RadarEngine.__init__ loads a matching version/mode record into approved_evidence; evaluate_candidate and Companion consume count/lower_ci without cohort provenance. The prospective report itself makes no automatic approval. Thus a comment that frozen prospective evidence is required is stronger than the actual approval writer/loader contract. This is a required correction before trusting strict entry approvals. It is not evidence of a forged approval in the retired DB, which was not queried.

Separate early-edge code has risk-set controls at treatmentT, immutable crossover pairs, identical observation-readiness floor, first qualifying pre-grad<85 canonical-unit treatment,100-signal/30-day stopping, normal and fixed-seed10,000 mint-connected-pair bootstrap. Strongest status requires finalized/matured complete matched numeric outcomes, zero unavailable and positive mean/comparison bootstrap lower limits; it does not unlock core/paper/trading. Missing quote never becomes−100%; automatic ECONOMIC_ZERO proof remains disabled. No real early cohort exists in the accepted context, and none was created or queried here. Four unmatched historical qualifying observations do not establish signal superiority or a usable N=100 acquisition rate.
